# Spaces

## Purpose

Workspace hubs grouping apps by space for teams and environments.

## How to get there

- Route: `/spaces`
- Nav: **Spaces**

## Operate from the console

1. Open `/spaces` and wait for live data.
2. Use filters, search, and row actions on the page.
3. Jump to related apps via inspector, Diagnose, or Spotlight (`⌘K`).
