# Health

## Purpose

Attention queue for broken and degraded apps with Diagnose and Zyra AI.

## How to get there

- Route: `/health`
- Nav: **Health**

## Operate from the console

1. Open `/health` and wait for live data.
2. Use filters, search, and row actions on the page.
3. Jump to related apps via inspector, Diagnose, or Spotlight (`⌘K`).
