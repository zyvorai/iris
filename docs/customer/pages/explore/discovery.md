# Discovery

## Purpose

Suggested apps from the cluster before publish.

## How to get there

- Route: `/discovery`
- Nav: **Discovery**

## Operate from the console

1. Open `/discovery` and wait for live data.
2. Use filters, search, and row actions on the page.
3. Jump to related apps via inspector, Diagnose, or Spotlight (`⌘K`).
